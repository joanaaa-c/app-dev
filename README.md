## About the Movie

**Avengers: Endgame** is a 2019 superhero movie from Marvel Studios, directed by **Anthony Russo** and **Joe Russo**. It is one of my favorite movies because it combines exciting action with an emotional story about *friendship, teamwork, courage, and sacrifice*.

## Main Characters

My favorite characters include:

- **Iron Man (Tony Stark)** — A genius inventor who always tries to protect others.
- **Captain America (Steve Rogers)** — A brave leader who stands by his friends.
- **Thor** — A powerful hero who learns to keep going despite his struggles.
- **Black Widow (Natasha Romanoff)** — A skilled and determined team member.
- **Hulk (Bruce Banner)** — A brilliant scientist with incredible strength.

## My Top 3 Scenes

1. **The time-travel mission** — The team works together to try to undo Thanos's actions.
2. **Captain America lifting Mjolnir** — A surprising moment that made the final battle even more exciting.
3. **Iron Man's final sacrifice** — An emotional ending that shows what being a hero can mean.
